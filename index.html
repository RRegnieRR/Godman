Info & Controls

W A S D move · Shift run · Mouse look
SPACE on ground: hold to charge, release to drive the arms down and ignite the palms and boots for a vertical launch. Hold again in the air to steer toward the crosshair.
The rounded meter has 16 stages over 5 seconds. Each stage stores more launch energy. Full charge can clear the atmosphere when aimed upward.
SPACE in the air: thrust toward the crosshair · aim up to climb, down to descend
The shoulder flashlight automatically lights the path ahead in darkness and follows your view.
C overview camera — zoom far out, orbit with the mouse, C again to return
F find a planet, star, black hole, nebula, or galaxy and smoothly inspect it
H hide/show orbit paths and all labels inside the universe (the HUD stays visible)
T lock onto the planet you are looking at — the camera tracks it and high-speed approaches brake for landing
Shift run on the ground · hold in flight to brake and maintain altitude. Release to coast
M instant boost to 39,937.7 km/h — Apollo 10, the fastest humans have ever travelled
R respawn on Earth · Esc open this menu

A real-proportion universe at 1:10 scale: true planet sizes, true orbital distances, dozens of real planetary systems sourced from NASA data, and a navigable 12-million-sample Milky Way, and 100 streamed 3D galaxies.
