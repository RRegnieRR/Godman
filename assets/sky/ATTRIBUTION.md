# Milky Way panorama

Credit **ESO/S. Brunier**. [The Milky Way panorama, eso0932a](https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/) is distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) per [ESO’s usage terms](https://www.eso.org/public/copyright/).

The game uses the publicly distributed 6000 × 3000 JPEG re-encoded as WebP. The renderer projects it as a directional sky near the Solar System and reduces its brightness for gameplay.
